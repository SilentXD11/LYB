Three things we look at closely

Most people do not need more clothes. They need clearer direction.

Proportion

Shoulders, waist and vertical balance. We find the cuts, lengths and necklines that work with your frame. Structure, never size.

Colour

Undertone, contrast and the shades that bring your face forward, so you know what to wear near your face and what to keep further away.

Wardrobe

A clear, practical plan: what to keep, what to add and how to put it together, so getting dressed takes minutes, not mornings.
